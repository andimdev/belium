# Belium — Timeline and Ages

This document tracks the current chronology.

## 1. Age anchors

### Chapter I opening

- Belium: **16**
- Cynthia: **14**

### First major time skip

After the Chapter I youth arc and Cynthia's fifth-badge period:

- **+4 years**
- Belium: ~20
- Cynthia: ~18

### Cynthia's mythology/search period

Cynthia spends approximately:

- **2.5 years**

studying, investigating, and following traces related to Belium/Cyrus while continuing her broader growth.

Approximate ages near the end of this period:

- Belium/Cyrus: ~22.5
- Cynthia: ~20.5

### League/training transition

After Cynthia obtains the eighth badge:

- she enters intense training;
- approximately **+5 years** pass before the Lucas/Maya era.

Approximate canonical-era ages:

- Cyrus: ~27.5 → treated as **28**
- Cynthia: ~25.5 → treated as **26**

This aligns with the intended canonical-age target.

# 2. Chapter I timeline

## Opening

Belium 16 / Cynthia 14.

Key early-state facts:

- Belium is intelligent, introverted, astronomy-focused.
- He likes battling.
- He believes in humanity and the soul.
- He cares deeply about Cynthia.
- Dragonair is his principal/only established early Pokémon in the central tragedy.

## Cynthia route progression

Known structure:

1. Cynthia becomes/acts as Professor Rowan's helper.
2. Begins her trainer journey.
3. Progresses through Gyms.
4. Reaches the **4th badge**.
5. Dragonair death event occurs.
6. Belium disappears/leaves without clear direction.
7. Houndour/rain period begins for Belium.
8. Flashbacks/related material include Zubat and Bagon.
9. Cynthia can continue her journey.
10. Reaches the **5th badge**.
11. First major story cut.
12. **+4 years**.

## Belium route

Playable and confirmed as a Chapter I route.

Exact route-exclusive sequence remains to be fully documented.

It should reveal information unavailable from Cynthia's perspective.

# 3. Chapter II timeline

## Four years later

Cynthia's interests have expanded into Sinnoh mythology.

Her journey as trainer has been partially paused/slowed by research, but her interest in battle remains.

She is also trying to find Belium.

## ~2.5-year search/investigation arc

Events include:

- following traces;
- mythology research;
- attempts to locate Belium/Cyrus;
- failing to meet him directly;
- seeing a Corvisquire watching her from a tree.

This Corvisquire later connects to Cyrus's Corviknight.

## Eighth badge

Cynthia eventually secures badge #8.

## Five-year training/transition

Cynthia undergoes intense League-focused training.

The game intentionally does not specify the exact point at which she becomes Champion.

## Lucas/Maya era

After ~5 years, perspective moves to Lucas/Maya.

Approximate ages at this point:

- Cyrus 28;
- Cynthia 26.

Lucas/Maya begin at an elevated power baseline (~Level 25).

Rowan sends them to investigate unusual Pokémon behavior at Mt. Coronet.

The adventure expands into Team Galactic conflict.

The arc ends at the Galactic HQ battle against Cyrus in Veilstone, immediately before his move toward Mt. Coronet/Spear Pillar.

# 4. Chapter III timeline

## Spear Pillar

Cyrus controls Dialga and Palkia and attempts to create his new world.

## Giratina intervention

Giratina drags Cyrus into the Distortion World.

## Distortion time

Time behaves abnormally.

Current narrative concept:

> ~1 second in the Distortion World can correspond to several minutes/hours in the normal world.

Exact duration and consequences are open for implementation tuning.

## Distortion relationship period

Cyrus and Giratina interact long enough for:

- mutual observation;
- Giratina's interest in Cyrus;
- Giratina's idea that Cyrus could help it escape;
- Giratina to sense Necrozma.

## Main Chapter III encounters

1. Exploration / perspective progression.
2. Cyrus confrontation.
3. Extremely difficult Cyrus battle.
4. Giratina boss battle.
5. Arrival of the remaining Lucas/Maya counterpart.
6. Solgaleo/Lunala revelation.
7. Necrozma fused-form battle.
8. Ultra Necrozma battle.
9. Interdimensional collapse.
10. Darkrai evacuation.
11. Cyrus/Belium sacrifice and death.

---

# 5. Postgame chronology

The postgame takes place after Cyrus's death.

It reconstructs previous hidden years via:

- Darkrai-led journeys to specific locations;
- memories/flashbacks;
- physical evidence such as Cyrus's diary;
- the yellow moon-shaped bookmark Cynthia once gave Belium.

The postgame should close unresolved story questions without changing the ending.
