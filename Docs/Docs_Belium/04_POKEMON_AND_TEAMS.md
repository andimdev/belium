# Belium — Pokémon and Teams

# 0. Cyrus's team-building philosophy

Cyrus's large roster is directly connected to the death of Dragonair.

## Belium at age 16

Belium loves battling but uses only Dragonair because Dragonair is the only Pokémon with whom he has formed a truly profound bond.

Dragonair is unusually strong for the point in the story (~Level 32) and can nearly defeat Mark's Mega Garchomp (~Level 25).

However, the Mt. Coronet tragedy exposes a structural weakness:

> A single exceptionally strong Pokémon is still a single point of failure.

## After Dragonair

Belium adopts a larger team for tactical reasons:

- type coverage;
- redundancy;
- multiple answers to threats;
- reduced dependence on one combatant;
- greater strategic flexibility.

At the same time, he tries to avoid reproducing the emotional vulnerability he experienced with Dragonair.

He therefore attempts to separate:

- **combat trust** from
- **deep emotional attachment**.

This distinction becomes part of Cyrus's philosophy.

The irony is that the separation repeatedly fails:

- Houndoom becomes one of his deepest bonds;
- Crobat proves friendship mechanically;
- Darkrai follows him voluntarily;
- Salamence, Corviknight and others become meaningful parts of his life.

Cyrus's roster is therefore both a tactical adaptation and an emotional defense mechanism.

# 2. Narrative meaning by Pokémon

## Mega Houndoom

### Origin

- encountered as Houndour after Dragonair's death;
- found in torrential rain;
- saved by Belium.

### Narrative role

One of Cyrus's two most emotionally important Pokémon.

Represents:

- survival;
- loyalty;
- Belium's continued capacity for compassion;
- a direct connection to the immediate aftermath of the Chapter I tragedy.

### Battle use

- deliberately absent in Galactic HQ;
- appears as a major alternative/final ace in the Distortion World battle;
- Mega Houndoom is expected to carry special narrative weight.

### Conditional Ultra Necrozma moment

If Cyrus wins the relevant battle branch:

- Mega Houndoom can participate beside Cyrus against Ultra Necrozma.

This becomes a final decisive battle built on their long-standing bond.

## Crobat

### Origin

- Zubat encountered by Belium in a cave;
- eventually evolves into Crobat.

### Core symbolism

Crobat evolves through friendship.

Its existence is direct mechanical evidence that Cyrus formed a deep bond despite later ideology.

### Galactic HQ

Crobat is Cyrus's **last Pokémon** in the battle.

This is intentional.

The ordering:

> Mega Metagross opens → Crobat closes

is designed as a narrative clue.

### Distortion World

Crobat will probably not be present in the final Cyrus battle.

Reason:

- Cyrus cares for it deeply;
- he does not want to expose Crobat to an excessively dangerous battle.

Its absence is protective.

Possible changes include:

- team composition;

- single vs double battle;
- AI difficulty;

A future design may decide whether exceptional flags can override this, but this is not currently required.

# 5. Team-resolution philosophy

Do **not** randomly choose six of ten.

The preferred direction is conditional composition based on:

- story route;
- narrative choices;
- character relationships;
- discovered/triggered events;
- possibly player battle tendencies;
- difficulty settings / challenge state.

The exact resolver algorithm is **TBD**.

## Optional implementation concept

A `CyrusTeamResolver` could receive:

- story flags;
- route state;
- battle telemetry;
- difficulty profile;
- narrative state;

and produce a validated authored team variant.

This should be deterministic and design-authored, not uncontrolled procedural randomness.
