# Belium — Characters

# 1. Belium / Cyrus

## Identity

- Birth/original name: **Belium**
- Later identity: **Cyrus**
- Age in Chapter I: 16
- Age in canonical-era events: ~28

## Young Belium — confirmed characterization

- introverted;
- academically exceptional;
- highly intelligent;
- fascinated by astronomy;
- **loves Pokémon battles**;
- believes in humanity;
- believes in the concept of the soul;
- quiet, analytical;
- highly competitive and genuinely passionate about Pokémon battles;
- keeps only Dragonair at age 16 because Dragonair is the only Pokémon with whom he has formed a truly deep and authentic bond;
- emotionally connected to Cynthia;
- secretly in love with her during youth;
- visually distinct from adult Cyrus.

## Visual design

- medium black hair;
- fringe/bangs that cover or partially cover the eyes.

Custom sprites are required.

## Arc

Belium's transformation is not a single event.

Dragonair's death is the main rupture, but multiple smaller experiences accumulate.

His later identity as Cyrus attempts to reject:

- emotion;
- attachment;
- the old self;
- the value of the imperfect world.

However, his Pokémon relationships repeatedly contradict that ideology.


## Battle philosophy before and after Dragonair

### Before Dragonair's death

Belium enjoys battling intensely.

His one-Pokémon team is an emotional choice, not a tactical ideal:

- Dragonair is his only true partner;
- their bond is unusually deep;
- Dragonair is well above the normal level curve;
- Belium has learned to win through the strength and trust of that single partnership.

### After Dragonair's death

Belium's thinking changes gradually.

He reaches two conclusions:

1. **Deep attachment creates unbearable vulnerability.**
2. **Relying on a single Pokémon creates tactical vulnerability.**

As he moves toward becoming Cyrus, he therefore:

- builds a larger roster;
- values redundancy, coverage and preparedness;
- tries to keep emotional distance from most partners;
- avoids admitting how important some Pokémon become to him.

This is a defining contradiction.

Cyrus's team is numerically larger because of Dragonair's death, while his emotional restraint is also a consequence of Dragonair's death.

The tragedy changes both his **heart** and his **team-building philosophy**.

## Emotional hierarchy among Pokémon

Strongest explicitly established emotional bonds:

- Houndoom;
- Crobat.

Darkrai is different because it is not owned.

## End state

Belium resurfaces emotionally/identity-wise during the ending arc.

Cyrus ultimately sacrifices himself and dies protecting others.

The final act should not be treated as automatic absolution.

# 2. Cynthia

## Age

- Chapter I: 14
- canonical-era target: ~26

## Early arc

- young trainer;
- works/assists with Professor Rowan;
- progresses through Gyms;
- knows Belium before he becomes Cyrus;
- is deeply affected by his disappearance;
- later becomes interested in Sinnoh mythology.

## Search arc

After the first time skip:

- investigates mythology;
- follows traces connected to Belium;
- searches for him for approximately 2.5 years;
- never reaches him directly;
- sees Corvisquire watching her.

## Growth

She does not exist only as a supporting character for Cyrus.

Her own trajectory includes:

- trainer development;
- research;
- mythology;
- Gym progression;
- eight badges;
- League training;
- eventual Champion status.

## Chapter III

After finally confirming that Cyrus is Belium, Cynthia does not want to leave him behind.

The reason should be framed primarily as:

- long-standing friendship;
- the years she spent searching;
- the fact that she has only just recovered the truth of who he is.

The finale does **not** require a romantic sacrifice framing.

# 3. Lucas / Maya

## Role

Playable protagonists in the later Chapter II / Chapter III era.

## Entry point

- begin at a higher-than-standard power baseline;
- around Level 25;
- sent by Professor Rowan to investigate unusual Pokémon behavior near Mt. Coronet.

## Narrative function

They know Cyrus primarily through:

- Team Galactic;
- contemporary conflict;
- direct confrontation.

Unlike Cynthia, they do not automatically know Belium's youth.

This distinction should affect dialogue.

## Chapter III

Depending on the chosen route:

- one is the currently active protagonist;
- the other later arrives with Solgaleo or Lunala.

# 6. Giratina

## Role

Not the secret villain.

## Character function

- observer;
- intrigued by Cyrus;
- sees him as unusually interesting;
- considers him potentially useful for escape from the Distortion World;
- develops a relationship with Cyrus during Chapter III;
- notices/senses Necrozma.

## Boss function

Participates in a special 1-vs-3-style encounter:

- Level ~100;
- uniquely boosted;
- unique boss ability/mechanic;
- not balanced like an ordinary catchable encounter.

## Final state

Established direction has Giratina remain in/with the Distortion World while others escape and Cyrus stays behind.

# 8. Darkrai

## Status

Not captured.

Not strictly a member in the ownership sense.

It follows Belium voluntarily.

## Origin

Weeks after Dragonair's death:

- Belium has severe nightmares;
- Darkrai senses his grief and pain;
- Darkrai finds him;
- chooses to remain near him.

Darkrai should not be treated as the original cause of Belium's trauma.

## Combat

Fights beside Cyrus.

Confirmed to appear as one of Cyrus's Pokémon/allies in the Galactic HQ battle.

## Ending

Darkrai understands Cyrus's intention.

It independently chooses to evacuate:

- Cynthia;
- Lucas;
- Maya;
- Cyrus's Pokémon;
- Solgaleo/Lunala.

This is possible precisely because Darkrai is a follower/companion, not property.

## Postgame

Darkrai becomes the central guide through Belium/Cyrus memories.

It leads the player to places rather than presenting memories from a menu.

# 9. Dragonair

## Role

Belium's only Pokémon at age 16 and the central loss of Chapter I.

Belium loves Pokémon battles, and Dragonair is the partner through whom he experiences that passion.

Dragonair is his only Pokémon because it is the only one with whom Belium has formed a bond he considers truly deep and genuine.

Dragonair is approximately Level 32 during the Mt. Coronet tragedy and nearly defeats Mark's Mega Garchomp despite fighting alone.

## Death

Dragonair dies protecting Belium after Mark orders Mega Garchomp to attack Belium directly.

Dragonair intercepts the attack, is grievously wounded, then is finished by Dragon Claw and Slash, with the final Slash cutting it in two.

Belium is then attacked with Strength, thrown into the cave rocks, and knocked unconscious.

He wakes hours later beside Dragonair's body.

## Consequences

The death becomes the primary rupture in Belium's youth.

It changes:

- his emotional philosophy;
- his willingness to form deep bonds;
- his team-building philosophy;
- his understanding of tactical vulnerability;
- his nightmares;
- Darkrai's arrival;
- later relationships;
- the meaning of Houndoom and Crobat;
- the final sacrifice.

Belium does not stop loving battle.

Instead, he learns to battle with more Pokémon while trying to care less deeply — a strategy that repeatedly fails emotionally.

# 10. Houndour / Houndoom

## Role

Found during torrential rain after Dragonair's death.

Belium saves it.

It becomes one of the most important bonds of his life.

Later becomes Mega Houndoom.

Houndoom is intentionally absent from the Galactic HQ battle.

That absence is narrative, not accidental.

In the final Distortion World battle, Mega Houndoom can be Cyrus's last/most emotionally significant alternative.

If Cyrus wins the relevant battle branch, Mega Houndoom may then fight beside him against Ultra Necrozma.

---

# 11. Zubat / Crobat

## Role

Found by Belium in a cave during the missing years.

Eventually evolves into Crobat.

Because Crobat evolves through friendship, its existence visibly contradicts Cyrus's claims about emotion/bonds.

## Galactic HQ

Crobat is Cyrus's **last Pokémon** in the HQ battle.

This is a deliberate clue to how much Cyrus values it.

## Final battle

Crobat may be absent because Cyrus does not want to expose it to such an extreme confrontation.

Its absence is protective, not emotional rejection.

---

# 12. Bagon / Salamence

Belium encounters Bagon repeatedly throwing itself from a cliff in an attempt to fly.

It eventually becomes Salamence.

Symbolically associated with:

- persistence;
- transformation;
- eventually achieving what once seemed impossible.

---

# 13. Corvisquire / Corviknight

Cynthia sees Corvisquire during the years she spends searching for Belium.

It watches her from a tree.

Later, Cyrus has Corviknight.

This allows the player to infer that:

- Belium/Cyrus knew where Cynthia was;
- he deliberately avoided direct contact.
