# Belium — Narrative Canon

This file contains the currently established story canon.

## Status legend

- **CONFIRMED CANON** — explicitly established.
- **OPEN / TBD** — unresolved.

# 1. Belium before Cyrus

## CONFIRMED CANON

Belium is Cyrus's original name.

At the beginning of the story:

- Belium is 16.
- Cynthia is 14.
- Belium is introverted.
- He is fascinated by astronomy.
- He **loves Pokémon battles**.
- He believes in humanity.
- He believes people have a soul.
- He is exceptionally intelligent and excels academically.
- He secretly loves Cynthia.
- Cynthia does not appear to recognize those feelings at that stage.
- His appearance differs from adult Cyrus: medium black hair with bangs/fringe.

Belium is not meant to start as an obviously evil or nihilistic character.

Dragonair is his only Pokémon at this stage, not because Belium avoids battle, but because Dragonair is the only Pokémon with whom he has formed a bond he considers truly deep and genuine.

His transformation is cumulative.

## CONFIRMED CANON — Dragonair tragedy

During the youth arc, a major event occurs inside Mt. Coronet involving **Mark**, a bully/rival obsessed with Cynthia.

### Battle setup

- Mark sends out **Garchomp**, approximately Level 25.
- Mark uses the Mega Stone and Garchomp becomes **Mega Garchomp**.
- Belium's Dragonair is approximately Level 32.
- Dragonair is stronger than the expected level curve and is capable of seriously threatening Mega Garchomp despite fighting alone.

### Sequence

1. Mark orders Mega Garchomp to use **Dragon Rush** (`Carga Dragón`).
2. Mega Garchomp charges Dragonair at extreme speed.
3. Dragonair dodges.
4. Dragonair counterattacks with **Dragon Tail** (`Cola Dragón`).
5. The counterattack leaves Mega Garchomp on the verge of defeat.
6. Mark loses control emotionally when he realizes he may lose.
7. Mark orders Mega Garchomp to use **Slash** (`Cuchillada`) directly against **Belium**, not Dragonair.
8. Dragonair places itself between Mega Garchomp and Belium to protect him.
9. Dragonair is gravely wounded.
10. Mega Garchomp follows with **Dragon Claw** (`Garra Dragón`).
11. Mega Garchomp then uses another **Slash** (`Cuchillada`) that cuts Dragonair in two and kills it.
12. Mark does not stop.
13. He orders Mega Garchomp to use **Strength** (`Fuerza`) against Belium.
14. Garchomp charges Belium with full force and throws him against the rocks inside the Mt. Coronet cave.
15. Belium is knocked unconscious.
16. Hours later, Belium wakes and finds Dragonair's lifeless body, cut in half.

### Narrative consequence

This is the principal rupture that accelerates Belium's collapse in faith, but it must **not** instantly transform him into Cyrus.

His philosophy changes gradually.

Before the tragedy:

- Belium loves Pokémon battles;
- he has only one Pokémon because Dragonair is the only partner with whom he shares a profound, authentic bond;
- he trusts that bond completely;
- Dragonair is also powerful enough that Belium has rarely been forced to confront the strategic weakness of relying on a single partner.

After the tragedy, Belium draws two different lessons from the same event.

#### Emotional lesson

He becomes afraid of forming equally deep bonds with future Pokémon because he does not want to suffer another loss of the same magnitude.

He does not stop caring.

Instead, he increasingly tries to **control how much he allows himself to care**.

#### Strategic lesson

Belium also recognizes that having only one Pokémon, even one significantly above the normal level curve, left him vulnerable.

Dragonair was extremely strong, but it was still fighting alone.

From this point onward, Belium becomes willing to maintain a larger team.

This creates the foundation for Cyrus's later approach:

- multiple strong Pokémon;
- broader tactical coverage;
- less dependence on a single combatant;
- deliberate attempts to avoid emotional dependence on any one partner.

The tragedy is therefore both:

- an emotional wound;
- and a major change in Belium's battle philosophy.

The irony is that this emotional distancing never fully succeeds.

Houndoom and Crobat in particular become deeply important to him, while Darkrai follows him through an entirely voluntary bond.

The game should frame Dragonair's death as the breaking point, not the completed transformation into Cyrus.

# 2. Belium's disappearance and early Pokémon encounters

## CONFIRMED CANON — Houndour

After Dragonair's death, Belium leaves without an apparent destination.

During a torrential rainstorm, he finds a Houndour in danger and saves it.

This Pokémon becomes one of the deepest relationships of his life and eventually becomes **Mega Houndoom**.

Houndoom is more than an ace: it is one of the clearest surviving connections to Belium.

## CONFIRMED CANON — Zubat

Belium later encounters a Zubat in a cave.

This Pokémon eventually becomes Crobat.

Crobat is especially important because the evolution mechanically implies strong friendship, directly contradicting Cyrus's later rhetoric about emotion and bonds.

## CONFIRMED CANON — Bagon

Belium encounters a Bagon repeatedly throwing itself from a cliff in its desire to fly.

This Bagon eventually becomes Salamence.

Its arc embodies growth and transformation: a creature repeatedly failing at an impossible goal eventually becomes capable of flight.

## CONFIRMED CANON — Darkrai

Weeks after Dragonair's death:

- Belium suffers terrible nightmares;
- Darkrai senses the pain, grief, sadness, and loss;
- Darkrai finds him;
- Darkrai is **not captured**;
- it voluntarily follows Belium and later fights beside Cyrus.

Darkrai is never strictly "owned" by Cyrus.

This becomes crucial to the ending.

# 3. Cynthia's path

## CONFIRMED CANON

Young Cynthia can be a playable protagonist.

Her early route includes:

- becoming/acting as Professor Rowan's assistant;
- beginning her journey as a trainer;
- progressing through Gyms;
- reaching the fourth badge before the Dragonair death event;
- continuing afterward;
- reaching the fifth badge before the first major time skip.

After the four-year skip:

- Cynthia has developed a serious interest in Sinnoh mythology;
- she studies it while temporarily slowing her trainer journey;
- she continues to care about battling;
- she searches for Belium, who is apparently missing;
- she follows traces/pistas related to him/Cyrus for approximately 2.5 years;
- she never catches him.

The closest known moment:

- Cynthia sees a **Corvisquire** watching her intently from the top of a tree.

The implication is that Cyrus/Belium knows where Cynthia is while deliberately avoiding direct contact.

After obtaining the eighth badge:

- Cynthia enters intense training for the Pokémon League;
- approximately five more years pass before the Lucas/Maya era.

The exact moment when Cynthia becomes Champion is intentionally not specified.

By the canonical-era portion, she is already Champion.

# 4. Chapter II — Lucas/Maya era

## CONFIRMED CANON

After Cynthia's five-year training/transition period, the story shifts to Lucas/Maya.

This new adventure does **not** begin with weak level-5 Pokémon.

Approximate starting power level:

- ~Level 25.

Professor Rowan sends the protagonist to monitor strange Pokémon behavior around Mt. Coronet.

This leads to encounters with Team Galactic.

Before/around this broader arc, the story already includes the relevant Cyrus appearance associated with Lake Valor.

The Lucas/Maya journey includes:

- a familiar Sinnoh adventure structure;
- stronger Gym Leaders than normal;
- exclusive events;
- a more advanced starting point;
- Team Galactic escalation;
- eventual confrontation with Cyrus at the Team Galactic HQ in Veilstone City.

The Chapter II climax is the Galactic HQ confrontation, after which Cyrus decides to go to Mt. Coronet to create his new world.

# 5. Chapter III — Spear Pillar and Distortion World

## CONFIRMED CANON

Cyrus reaches the point where he controls Dialga and Palkia in an attempt to create his new world.

Giratina intervenes and Cyrus is dragged into the Distortion World.

## Distortion time

The project concept includes extreme time distortion:

- approximately one second in the Distortion World can correspond to days/weeks in the normal world.

Exact mathematical behavior remains a gameplay/narrative design detail.

## Cyrus and Giratina

During Cyrus's time in the Distortion World:

- their relationship develops;
- Giratina finds Cyrus extremely interesting;
- Giratina sees Cyrus as potentially useful for escaping the Distortion World;
- Giratina notices signs/presence related to Necrozma.

Giratina is not secretly evil.

## Player perspective

The chapter allows another perspective choice:

- continue from Lucas/Maya;
- or move to Cynthia.

Exact switching rules remain to be fully specified.

## Cyrus battle

A heated confrontation with Cyrus leads to an extremely difficult battle.

The outcome can influence subsequent presentation/conditions, but does not prevent the canonical sequence.

## Giratina battle

After the Cyrus confrontation:

- the Chapter III protagonists unite;
- Cyrus steps aside and smiles triumphantly;
- Giratina enters a 1-vs-3 style boss encounter;
- Giratina is approximately Level 100;
- Giratina has a unique boss-oriented ability/mechanic;
- the fight is intentionally much harder than a conventional wild Legendary battle.

## Necrozma reveal

The remaining Lucas/Maya counterpart arrives:

- if Lucas was selected, Maya arrives;
- if Maya was selected, Lucas arrives.

They arrive with either:

- Solgaleo;
- or Lunala;

depending on the established choice/route.

They warn that the true orchestrator behind the larger events is **Necrozma**.

Necrozma absorbs Solgaleo/Lunala and enters the corresponding fused form.

A battle follows that is harder than Giratina's.

After defeat:

- Necrozma expels Solgaleo/Lunala;
- Necrozma becomes **Ultra Necrozma**.

Ultra Necrozma threatens everything by opening an interdimensional rupture capable of consuming the Distortion World and, by consequence, the normal world.

# 6. Final sacrifice

## CONFIRMED CANON

Cyrus/Belium sacrifices himself.

He leaves his Pokémon behind.

The ending does not contain a survival route.

Cynthia does not want to abandon him.

Her refusal is rooted in the friendship they built at the beginning of the story, not in the need for a romantic sacrifice.

Cyrus's Pokémon do not want to abandon him either.

Houndoom and Crobat are especially emotionally important to him.

## Darkrai's role

Darkrai understands the situation through a final exchange/look with Cyrus.

Because Darkrai is not captured and has always acted voluntarily, it can make an independent choice.

Darkrai becomes the **savior of the survivors**.

It carries/evacuates, inside a large dark-energy sphere or equivalent manifestation:

- Cynthia;
- Lucas;
- Maya;
- Cyrus's Pokémon;
- Solgaleo/Lunala.

This prevents them from remaining behind with Cyrus.

The evacuation is not voluntary for everyone; several characters/Pokémon would otherwise refuse to leave.

## Giratina

Established direction:

- Giratina remains tied to the Distortion World;
- Cyrus/Belium's final act is connected to Giratina and the closing/collapse event.

The earlier established ending concept has Cyrus and Giratina remaining behind while the others escape.

# 7. Necrozma foreshadowing

## CONFIRMED CANON

Necrozma's role must be foreshadowed before the reveal.

In Chapter I, a mysterious Pokémon observes Belium during specific events through dark dimensional cracks.

The player should naturally assume the observer is Giratina.

The entity should be shown ambiguously:

- partial silhouette;
- eyes;
- fragment of body;
- dimensional distortion;
- no clear full reveal.

Chapter III reveals that Necrozma was connected to these observations.

## OPTIONAL PROPOSALS previously discussed

Possible visual motifs:

- strange stars/constellations;
- prismatic darkness;
- unusual meteorite/prism imagery;
- eclipse imagery;
- dimensional cracks.

These are not all confirmed canon.

# 8. Cyrus's death

## CONFIRMED CANON — immutable

Cyrus dies.

The postgame must **not** undermine this with:

- fake survival hints;
- mysterious silhouettes implying he escaped;
- hidden alternate endings;
- resurrection routes.

The purpose of the postgame is to reveal who he was, not to reverse the ending.

---

# 9. Themes

## CONFIRMED / strongly established themes

- identity versus self-erasure;
- bonds that contradict ideology;
- grief;
- transformation;
- memory;
- friendship across long separation;
- the difference between understanding and absolution;
- sacrifice;
- the person behind "Cyrus";
- the making of Cynthia;
- the persistence of Belium inside Cyrus.

## OPTIONAL recurring motif discussed

Astronomy/stars can be used as a recurring symbolic motif.

Potential thematic ideas discussed:

- stars dying;
- stars no longer being visible without truly being erased;
- the same musical motif evolving from Belium to Cyrus and later returning in altered/restored form.

These remain creative directions, not fixed dialogue.
