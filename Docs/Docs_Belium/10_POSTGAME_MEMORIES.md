# Belium — Postgame Memories

# 1. Purpose

The postgame exists to **complete the history of Belium/Cyrus**, not to change his fate.

Cyrus is dead.

The postgame reveals missing years and resolves questions left deliberately incomplete during the three chapters.

---

# 2. Darkrai as guide

Darkrai is the protagonist/central mechanism of the memory system.

The player does **not** open a memory menu and click a button.

Instead:

1. Darkrai appears.
2. Darkrai moves/leads.
3. The player follows it through the world.
4. They reach a meaningful location.
5. Darkrai uses its power.
6. A flashback/memory is triggered.

This keeps memories grounded in geography and exploration.

---

# 3. Why Darkrai can do this

Darkrai:

- found Belium shortly after Dragonair's death;
- followed him voluntarily;
- was present during parts of his missing life;
- was never captured;
- survived the final catastrophe;
- saved the others.

It is uniquely positioned to reveal events no living human fully witnessed.

---

# 4. Confirmed memory topics

Postgame memories should include material such as:

- how Belium met his other Pokémon;
- Houndour/Houndoom development;
- Zubat/Crobat history;
- Bagon/Salamence history;
- Impidimp/Grimmsnarl history;
- Corvisquire/Corviknight and Cynthia surveillance;
- Mega Metagross history;
- the first battle in which Houndoom Mega Evolves;
- adventures in northern Sinnoh;
- how Belium/Cyrus avoided being found by Cynthia;
- other significant events from the missing years.

Exact order remains TBD.

---

# 5. Diary sequence

## CONFIRMED CANON

Cynthia eventually finds Cyrus's personal diary.

Important details:

- the cover still bears the name **Belium**;
- inside is the yellow moon-shaped bookmark Cynthia gave Belium.

The diary proves that:

- Cyrus did not completely erase the old identity;
- he preserved at least one deeply personal artifact tied to Cynthia and youth.

The scene should not imply that this erases his responsibility for later actions.

---

# 6. Yellow moon bookmark

This object is intentionally ordinary.

It does not need magical power.

Its value comes from:

- memory;
- continuity;
- preservation;
- proof that Belium remained somewhere inside Cyrus.

---

# 7. Memory presentation

## CONFIRMED

Memories are discovered through Darkrai at locations.

## OPEN / TBD

The memory itself may be:

- cinematic;
- partially interactive;
- fully playable mini-sequence;
- a mix depending on memory importance.

A previously discussed optional idea is to temporarily control Belium inside some memories.

This is not yet mandatory.

---

# 8. Memory categories

Recommended implementation split:

## Main memories

Reveal essential canon and should ultimately be discoverable by any completed save.

## Contextual memories

Can depend on:

- route choices;
- previously seen scenes;
- character knowledge;
- optional events;
- replay state.

This preserves replay value without hiding essential story resolution behind a full replay requirement.

---

# 9. Postgame emotional arc

The postgame should move from:

- curiosity;
- reconstruction;
- missing years;
- Pokémon bonds;
- Cynthia's unanswered questions;

toward:

- understanding why he stayed away;
- understanding how "Belium" persisted;
- acceptance that he is gone.

Darkrai should not become a vehicle for resurrection.

---

# 10. Darkrai's final thematic function

Darkrai begins by following a traumatized teenager because it senses his nightmares.

At the end of the main story, Darkrai leaves him only because it must save the others.

In the postgame, it preserves his history.

This gives Darkrai a full arc:

```text
Witness
→ Companion
→ Fighter
→ Savior
→ Keeper of Memory
```
